ADMISSION STEPS

4 Simple Steps to Join Our School

Our transparent and merit-based admission process is designed to be straightforward and welcoming.

STEP 1

1. Submit Application

Complete the online admission form or collect a prospectus from either campus administrative office.

STEP 2

2. Document Verification

Submit required academic transcripts, birth certificates, and identification documents.

STEP 3

3. Assessment & Interview

Students participate in an age-appropriate friendly assessment and a family interaction session.

STEP 4

4. Enrollment Confirmation

Finalize enrollment by completing fee formalities and receiving official classroom assignments.

ELIGIBILITY & AGE CRITERIA

Admission Eligibility

We seek enthusiastic learners who demonstrate curiosity, good conduct, and a passion for growth.

Consult Grade Placement
Early Years Intake

Play Group & Nursery

Children aged 3+ to 4+ years. Focuses on foundational social interaction, sensory discovery, motor skills development, and joyful play-based learning.

Elementary Progression

Primary School (Grades 1 to 5)

Children aged 6+ years for Grade 1. Requires successful completion of pre-primary competencies or satisfactory evaluation in literacy and foundational numeracy.

Advanced Preparation

Secondary School (Grades 6 to 10)

Students seeking entry into middle and senior years. Admission requires formal academic transcripts from their previous accredited institution and merit assessment.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Checklist of Documents

Please prepare attested copies of the following documents prior to verification.

Birth Certificate

Official computerized government-issued online birth certificate.

Passport Photographs

4 recent color passport-size photographs of student and 2 of each parent.

Academic Records

Report cards and official transcripts from the previous academic year.

Parent NID

Photocopy of parents' National Identity Cards (NID) or valid passports.

Transfer Certificate

Official school leaving clearance / transfer certificate signed by the head of previous institution.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

General Inquiries (FAQ)

Common questions and answers regarding our admission policies.

When does the admission process begin? +
Admission form distribution for the new academic year typically commences in mid-October.
Does the school provide transportation? +
Yes, we offer secure, GPS-tracked school bus services covering major routes.
What is the average student-teacher ratio? +
We strictly maintain a 25:1 student-teacher ratio across classrooms.
Are scholarships available for meritorious students? +
Yes, we provide merit-based tuition waivers and financial assistance.
How can parents track student academic progress? +
Parents receive digital progress updates via our portal and participate in quarterly interaction sessions.
